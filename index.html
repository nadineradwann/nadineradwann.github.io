About Me

Shawn Garcia is an art director who brings ideas to life through thoughtful visual storytelling. With a background spanning design, illustration, and creative strategy, she specializes in turning abstract concepts into clear, engaging visuals.

Her work blends creativity with structure, balancing originality and clarity to deliver designs that resonate with audiences. Passionate about collaboration and continuous learning, Shawn approaches every project as an opportunity to connect people through design.

Process

RESEARCH & DISCOVERY

Start by understanding the client's goals, audience, and the story behind the project. This stage helps build a strong creative foundation.

BRAINSTORM & SKETCH

Explore different ideas and directions through rough sketches and notes. This creative phase turns abstract concepts into possible visual solutions.

DEVELOP & PROTOTYPE

Translate sketches into digital mockups or prototypes, testing layout, colors, and visual flow to see how ideas work in practice.

REFINE & FINALIZE

Gather feedback and make thoughtful adjustments. The design is refined until it clearly communicates the intended message and feels polished.
